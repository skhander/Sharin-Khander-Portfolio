---
name: Portfolio work direction
description: The user's preferred balance of product evidence, project imagery, and brand palette on the portfolio homepage.
---

Prefer a vertical project sequence over a conventional multi-column card grid. Keep the alternating imagery and varying aspect ratios, but make concrete product problems, decisions, and supported results prominent enough to be scanned quickly. Do not rely on a "Product builder" label alone to establish that identity. Keep headlines and supporting copy short, specific, and non-repetitive; avoid stacks of abstract slogans or oversized typography substituting for the work.

**Why:** The user said the vertical list was cleaner and liked the alternating sequence for its image aspect ratios. They later said the portfolio still read as a "design girlie, magazine editor" rather than a product builder, despite an explicit builder label. In reviewing a ShopMy-inspired mockup, they objected to too much typography and repetitive, empty wording, asking for cleaner and punchier product writing.

**How to apply:** Preserve authentic imagery, navigation, filters, and the green, pink, gold, and oxblood palette. Lead with documented product work and trade-offs; avoid invented impact, ownership, or launch claims when a case study is thin. This is project-specific, not a general preference for unrelated apps.
