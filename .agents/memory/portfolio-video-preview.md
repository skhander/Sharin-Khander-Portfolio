---
name: Portfolio video preview
description: Why case-study walkthroughs use a click-to-play poster before mounting video.
---

For large case-study walkthrough videos, show a static poster with an explicit play control and mount the video only when selected.

**Why:** The browser preview showed a loading spinner over the poster even with video preloading disabled. A separate poster keeps the first impression readable and avoids eager video requests.

**How to apply:** When adding or replacing walkthrough media on portfolio pages, preserve accessible playback controls after the poster is selected and verify the initial view at desktop and mobile widths.
