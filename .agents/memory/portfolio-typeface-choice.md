name: Portfolio typeface choice
description: Why the portfolio uses Manrope instead of the earlier serif or Avenir directions.
---

Use Manrope throughout the portfolio, including display text and small metadata. Keep the existing white background and accent colors when making typography-only refinements.

**Why:** The user rejected the serif direction. They do not have a licensed Avenir font file and explicitly chose Google Fonts Manrope rather than a device-dependent Avenir stack.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce serif families or references to local Avenir fonts. When adjusting hierarchy, use Manrope weights, sizes, line heights, and spacing without changing copy or layout.
