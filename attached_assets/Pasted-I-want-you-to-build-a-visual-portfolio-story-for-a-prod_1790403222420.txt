I want you to build a visual portfolio story for a product I worked on at The RealReal.

Do NOT build a traditional PM case study.

Do NOT create sections called “Problem / Solution / My Role / Results.”

Do NOT turn this into a deck.

Tell one product story from beginning to end, with the interface and interactions doing most of the explaining.

The core idea:

**We wanted The RealReal to become a place where people discover their next favorite designer — not just a place where they shop for designers they already know.**

The story:

80% of visitors to designer pages left without clicking a product.

Our hypothesis wasn't simply that the page needed better merchandising.

It was that when the products on a designer page didn't resonate, we gave shoppers no meaningful next step.

So we asked:

**What if a designer page could become a doorway into discovering another designer?**

The inspiration is how Spotify helps people move from artists they already like to artists they haven't discovered yet.

We wanted to create the fashion equivalent:

**The Row → Lemaire → Jil Sander → Toteme**

Build the experience around that idea.
