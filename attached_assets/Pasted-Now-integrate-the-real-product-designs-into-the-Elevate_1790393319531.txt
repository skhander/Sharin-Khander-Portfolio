Now integrate the real product designs into the **Elevated Experience** project page.

The storytelling should be **extremely concise, visual, and scannable**. Think editorial product story, not a traditional PM case study.

The hiring manager should understand the story by scrolling through the page in ~60 seconds.

### Story structure

Use this sequence:

**1. The starting point**

Show the **PDP Before** screenshot large.

Caption:

> **The PDP felt anything but luxurious.**
> Small imagery. Too much whitespace. Not enough product.

Then introduce the experiment:

> **We tested two ways to make the product more immersive.**

Show the two designs side-by-side:

**A — Grid**
**B — Immersive scroll**

Keep the labels extremely small.

---

### 2. A small change. A big lesson.

Show the variation where the **Obsessions heart moved to the left of the product details.**

Large metric:

**−15% Obsessions**
**−10% Orders / visitor**

Then one sharp takeaway:

> **Don't make users look for muscle memory.**

Supporting copy:

> We moved the heart. Users stopped using it. We shipped the fix the same day.

Do not add more explanation.

---

### 3. The winning experience

Show the **immersive scroll** design prominently, ideally with a short video or interaction.

Headline:

> **Good friction can still be good UX.**

Supporting copy:

> The third image took an extra scroll to reach—but the more immersive experience won.

Large metric:

**+5% Orders / visitor**

Make this feel like a product decision, not a metric dump.

---

### 4. Then we took the same idea to Browse.

Transition with a simple statement:

> **Next, we brought the same thinking to PLPs.**

Show a large **Before → After** comparison.

Before:

* cluttered filters
* tiny product images

After:

* filters behind a drawer
* larger product imagery
* more visual breathing room

---

### 5. The risk

Make the product judgment explicit:

> **This could have backfired.**

Then three very short points:

**More clicks**
Filtering now required an extra tap.

**6M+ items**
Shoppers rely on filters to find what they want.

**Bigger images**
More visual browsing could slow high-throughput shoppers.

Then the result:

**+10% Conversion**

And one final takeaway:

> **More immersive browsing beat faster browsing.**

---

# Overall writing style

The copy should feel:

* punchy
* intelligent
* confident
* extremely concise
* easy to skim
* conversational
* grounded in actual product decisions

Use **short sentences.**
Use **large headlines.**
Use **metrics as visual anchors.**
Use **screenshots to explain the product.**
